    For buyers

    How to order

    1. Choose your pieces

      Add catalogue styles and quantities to your quote list. For a piece you have in mind, send a sourcing brief with a reference photo.

    2. Request a quote

      Send your list with your contact details. We’ll check the specifications, available quantities and wholesale price for your request.

    3. Confirm your order

      Review the written quote, payment terms and delivery estimate. Reply to Juno when you’re ready to proceed.

    Can I mix styles or request a sample?

    Add each style and its quantity to your list. If you’d like a sample, mention the style code in your enquiry. We’ll check sample availability and minimum quantities for those pieces.

    How are payment and delivery arranged?

    Include your delivery postcode and any date you’re working towards. We’ll confirm the freight cost, expected timing and payment terms with you before you decide to order.
